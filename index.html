HOW IT WORKS

Know what is being arranged before you approve a repair.

Taporaa is intended to make it easier to discover a suitable provider and understand the next steps. The provider carries out the repair and should explain the estimate, parts, timeline and any applicable warranty directly to you.

Read the process →
  1. 01

    Describe the issue

    Share your vehicle type, location and the symptoms you have noticed.

  2. 02

    Check provider availability

    Confirm that a suitable independent provider can take the request.

  3. 03

    Review the estimate

    Ask about labour, parts, additional charges and expected completion time.

  4. 04

    Approve the work

    Proceed only after you understand and accept the provider's terms.
