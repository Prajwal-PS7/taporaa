THE PROCESS

From a vehicle problem to a clearer next step.

Taporaa's intended service-discovery flow helps customers explain what they need and confirm the important details with the independent provider.

  1. 01

    Describe your vehicle and issue

    Be ready to share your vehicle type, make and model, current location, a short description of the problem and whether the vehicle can be moved safely.

  2. 02

    Check whether a provider is suitable

    A relevant provider must confirm that the requested service is within their capabilities and that they can attend or accept the vehicle. Availability can change by area, time and workload.

  3. 03

    Ask for a clear estimate

    Confirm inspection charges, labour, parts, taxes or other fees, expected timing and whether additional work may require separate approval.

  4. 04

    Approve the repair directly

    Proceed only when you understand the provider's estimate and terms. Keep the invoice, job details and any warranty information supplied by the provider.

  5. 05

    Keep the service record

    Retain messages, receipts and details of parts installed. Contact the provider named on your invoice if you have a question about workmanship or a provider-issued warranty.

Is every request an instant booking?

No. A website enquiry or expression of interest is not itself a confirmed booking. The provider must confirm availability and the customer must agree to the scope and price. Do not hand over a vehicle or make a payment based only on an unconfirmed service listing.
