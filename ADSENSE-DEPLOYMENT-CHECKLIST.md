# Before you redeploy and request AdSense review

## Business identity and trust
- [ ] Enter the exact legal operator name in `assets/js/site-config.js`.
- [ ] Add a monitored public business email; add a real business phone/address only if you want them public.
- [ ] Make sure the Contact page gives visitors a working way to reach the operator.
- [ ] Review the About page so the service-aggregator/independent-provider model reflects the real business.
- [ ] Add accurate service-area and provider-selection details only when verifiable.
- [ ] Never add fabricated testimonials, ratings, partners, workshop addresses, prices or service guarantees.

## Privacy and compliance
- [ ] Review the Privacy Policy against the actual GA4, AdSense, hosting, email and contact setup.
- [ ] Confirm the privacy contact email works and receives requests.
- [ ] Confirm any cookie consent / CMP requirements for the countries you target; a privacy page alone is not a consent-management system.
- [ ] Review Terms of Use and Editorial Policy for accuracy and legal suitability.
- [ ] Keep ads off Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms and other utility pages; do not make ads resemble navigation or page controls.

## Technical checks
- [ ] Homepage loads over HTTPS and HTTP redirects to HTTPS.
- [ ] `https://taporaa.com/robots.txt` is reachable and does not block Google crawlers.
- [ ] `https://taporaa.com/sitemap.xml` returns valid XML and all listed URLs return HTTP 200.
- [ ] `https://taporaa.com/ads.txt` returns the verified publisher line.
- [ ] All navigation, internal links, images and mobile menu work.
- [ ] The About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Editorial Policy pages are linked from the footer.
- [ ] Remove the old dummy telephone number and obsolete anchor links.
- [ ] Check AdSense crawler access and the AdSense tag in the live page source.

## Request another review
- [ ] In AdSense → Sites, open `taporaa.com` and read the detailed status/reason.
- [ ] In Policy Center, resolve every item marked as a policy issue.
- [ ] Recheck the website as a logged-out visitor and on mobile.
- [ ] Request review only after the required details above are complete.

Google does not publish a guaranteed approval threshold based on article count or word count. Focus on genuinely useful, original content and a complete visitor experience.
