SERVICE CATEGORIES

Explore common vehicle-service needs.

These categories describe the types of help customers may look for through Taporaa. An independent provider must confirm capability, availability, price and scope for each request.

01

Puncture and tyre repair

A puncture, slow leak or damaged tyre can make a vehicle unsafe to ride. A provider may inspect the tyre, assess whether a repair is suitable, and explain whether replacement is needed. Not every puncture can be safely repaired; the decision depends on tyre type, damage location, condition and manufacturer guidance.

Before requesting help

  • Move away from moving traffic and do not continue riding on a visibly flat or damaged tyre.
  • Share your exact pickup location and whether the vehicle is in a safe place.
  • Ask whether the provider can inspect the tyre on-site or whether transport to a workshop is needed.
02

Engine and mechanical repair

Unusual noise, starting difficulty, loss of power, vibration, smoke or a warning light can have several causes. A mechanic needs to inspect the vehicle before confirming a diagnosis or estimate. A short description of when the symptom occurs can help the provider prepare.

Useful details to share

  • Vehicle make, model, approximate year and fuel type.
  • When the symptom started and whether it happens continuously or intermittently.
  • Any warning light, visible leak, smoke, unusual smell or recent repair.
03

Accessories and spare parts

Replacement parts and accessories must match the vehicle and intended use. Before approving a purchase or fitment, confirm the part number or compatibility, whether it is genuine or aftermarket, the total price, installation charges and the seller's warranty or return terms.

Questions to ask

  • Is the part compatible with my exact model and variant?
  • Is it new, refurbished, genuine or aftermarket?
  • What warranty, invoice and return conditions apply?
04

Two-wheeler towing and recovery

When a motorcycle or scooter cannot be ridden safely, a suitable recovery provider may transport it to a workshop or another agreed destination. Availability, vehicle capacity, pickup time and charges must be confirmed before dispatch.

Before dispatch

  • Share your pickup pin or a clear landmark and the destination.
  • Describe the vehicle and the reason it cannot be ridden.
  • Confirm the total charge, loading method and delivery arrangements.
05

Doorstep inspection and minor assistance

Some providers may offer selected checks or minor work at the customer's location. Not every repair is appropriate for a roadside or home visit. Ask the provider what can be completed safely on-site and what must be done at a workshop.

06

Workshop-based service

Complex diagnostics, equipment-dependent work and repairs requiring a controlled environment may need a visit to the provider's workshop. Confirm the workshop address, estimated completion time, parts requirements and any inspection or storage charges before leaving the vehicle.

Important service note

Taporaa's service categories are informational. They do not promise that a provider is available, that a repair can be completed at a particular location, or that a specific price or turnaround time applies. Obtain a written or message-based estimate from the provider before work begins.
